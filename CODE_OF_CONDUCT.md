# Code of Conduct

TEMOA-Italy adopts the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1. By participating in this repository you agree to abide by it.

In short: be respectful and constructive. Harassment, personal attacks, and publishing others' private information are not tolerated. Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject any contribution that violates these terms, and may restrict participation in serious or repeated cases.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to [Matteo Nicoli](mailto:matteo.nicoli@polito.it). Reports are reviewed promptly and treated confidentially.

Members of Politecnico di Torino also remain subject to the university's Code of Ethics (*Codice Etico*) and to its procedures on harassment and misconduct, which take precedence over this document. Where a report concerns the maintainer, or where you would rather not address it here, those university procedures are the appropriate route.
